Cleared 0% of the lawn · goal 60%

Tess has landed on a lawn of E. coli. Every cell she infects fills up with purple copies of her. Tap a cell to burst it, and its copies infect the cells around it: the fuller it is, the more it infects. But every infected cell is getting ready to self-destruct, and turns red as it gets close. Burst it in time, or it takes its copies with it, and nothing can get past it. Clear 60% of the lawn to win! Keyboard: space bursts the cell closest to self-destructing.
