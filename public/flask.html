Did you know?

Level 1 · 0 / 28 cells

Use the arrow keys to steer Flo. Touch the flask and slide your finger to steer Flo. She can only get into cells with teal bumps. Each one she gets into releases more copies of her, which drift off and get into cells nearby on their own. But cells warn their neighbors (yellow outline): copies can’t get into a warned cell, only Flo can, and it takes her longer. Take 28 cells to win, and don’t touch the antibodies. They keep spreading, so be quick!
